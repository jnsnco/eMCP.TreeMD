Welcome to the WorkOS tool!


Available routes include:
- **hosted_ui**: Customizable hosted UI for authentication at any size
- **enterprise_auth**: Single sign-on, passkeys, social login, passwords, and more
- **roles_and_permissions**: Advanced RBAC with custom roles and role assignment
- **radar**: Protect your app from bots, fraud, and abuse
- **widgets**: Complete functionality for common enterprise app workflows
- **custom_metadata**: Store additional information about users and organizations
